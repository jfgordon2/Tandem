QUESTIONS
Can I turn it off?Not with a switch: comma asks for drives over Wi-Fi on its own, separately from Storage & Data › Uploads. Save Data pauses it on a network, and cellular sends nothing unless Uploads on Cellular is on.
Does it matter how or where I drive?No, just drive as you normally would.
Are all of my drives uploaded?No, comma pulls a selection of them.
What's a good USB-C charger?Any fast phone or laptop charger is fine.
Does it matter which software I run?Yes. Only openpilot and some forks can be used for training.
