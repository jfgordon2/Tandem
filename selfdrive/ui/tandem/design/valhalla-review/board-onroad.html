Driving: fresh guidance (reference)

An active local route has fresh, valid GPS/instructions.

Driving: turn card expires separately

Offline routing is on and the card's NavInstructionState timestamp is missing, malformed, in the future or older than 2.5 seconds. In this example navInstruction on the message bus is still fresh.

Driving: gps

Effective offline routing is on, a destination is set, and navInstruction is invalid or last arrived more than 2.5 seconds ago. The active route has no usable live GPS fix, or its timestamp is older than 2.5 seconds or in the future.

Driving: rerouting

Effective offline routing is on, a destination is set, and navInstruction is invalid or last arrived more than 2.5 seconds ago. The car is off the route or its bearing disagrees with the route, and a replacement is being calculated.
